ABOUT / 01

Technology excites me most when it fixes everyday frustrations.

I’m Abdul-Rehman, a BS Computer Science student at Qarshi University in Lahore, currently in Semester 5. I focus on building web projects that solve real problems, bringing front-end and back-end development together with practical problem-solving.

At university, timetable clashes can make the first weeks of a semester chaotic. That challenge inspired me to build a Timetable Generator. I’m also developing PrintSafe around a question I care about: how can people process sensitive files with more privacy and transparency?

I’m interested in making digital tools feel clear and dependable. Each project is a chance to learn by doing, test an idea against a real need, and make the result easier for someone else to use.
